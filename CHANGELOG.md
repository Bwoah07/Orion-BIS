# Orion BiS

## 2.3.1
- The glow behind the Orion alert is stronger: a brighter flash, then it stays visible and breathes.
- Fixed the "AchievementShield_OnLoad" warning the achievement toast caused.

## 2.3.0
- New default alert look: a clean Orion box with a soft glow behind it, that drops into place. The
  achievement-style toast is still there: Settings → Alert style.
- Alerts can be moved: Settings → Move alerts, drag the gold box, right-click when done. Reset position puts
  them back.
- Up to three alerts show at once, newest on top. Hover one to keep it up; right-click to dismiss.

## 2.2.3
- Every loot alert is now an achievement toast, and it stays up for 15 seconds (longer while you hover it).
  Your items show "★ YOUR BiS/UPGRADE/MINOR"; others show who wants them. Hover for the item tooltip.
- The old alert box is only used if the toast isn't available.

## 2.2.2
- Items on your list now get a real achievement toast (the same frame, glow and shine as an earned
  achievement) and the achievement sound.
- The loot alert is plain again: no yellow glow. The glow on roll and loot windows is softer.

## 2.2.1
- The gold glow now sits around items instead of over them, so names and buttons stay readable.

## 2.2.0
- Drop alerts for your items now pop in like an achievement: a gold glow, a shine across, and the achievement
  sound. Pick another sound (Epic loot, Raid warning) in Settings. Alerts fade out instead of vanishing and
  stay up while you hover them.
- Items on your list flash gold in the need/greed roll window and in the loot window, with a "YOUR BiS"
  badge, so you don't pass on them by mistake. Can be turned off in Settings.
- Loot tab cleanup: one BiS / Upgrade / Minor bar per item instead of three buttons, item levels, items on
  your list get a coloured stripe, quality-coloured icon edges, gear listed before mounts and decor, and a
  cleaner boss list.

## 2.1.0
- Pick the difficulty in Loot: LFR, Normal, Heroic or Mythic for raids; Normal, Heroic or Mythic for dungeons.
  Item links and tooltips show that difficulty's item level.
- New Mythic+ view: this season's Mythic+ dungeons, boss by boss, at Mythic+ item levels.
- The logo is gone from the window header (still on the minimap button and in the addon list).

## 2.0.1
- Fixed blank tabs and buttons on first open (text set before the font had loaded).

## 2.0.0
- New **Loot** tab: every raid and dungeon, boss by boss, read live from the Adventure Guide. Click BiS, Upgrade or Minor
  on any item to add it. Filter by your spec, your class or everything. Works for any expansion and every new season.
- New look: custom dark and gold design, Inter font, own icons. No Blizzard frame textures.
- **My list** grouped by priority, with where each item drops and who else wants it. Export your list as a code.
- **Guild** tab with search and a hide-received filter.
- **Settings** tab: alerts, sound, tooltips, minimap button, window size, guild sharing, clear list.
- Minimap button and addon compartment entry.
- Translated into German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Korean and Chinese (simplified and traditional).
- Guild data from a website lives in a separate private addon (OrionBiS_GuildData), never in the public download.
- Sync protocol unchanged, so 1.x users in the guild keep syncing.

## 1.1.0
- Lists shared with your guild automatically, tooltips show who wants an item, gold drop alert.
